# Workflow Review

Tracewright examines whether an AI-assisted process preserves meaning, accountability and practical human intervention. It organises evidence for review; it does not certify a system, rank people or direct a live operation.

## The intended sequence

1. Describe purpose, boundaries, affected people and unacceptable outcomes.
2. Supply a normal path and known exceptions. Leave unknown conditions unknown.
3. Confirm the AI-prepared structure with the user before detailed assessment.
4. Compare policy, configuration, tests and operational records, preserving versions and source roles.
5. Create source-linked observations with alternatives, limits and next checks.
6. A qualified accountable reviewer decides what to investigate or change.
7. Record implementation, controlled retesting and operational verification as separate later states.

The current browser intake implements registration and request preparation. The example reviews are already prepared. It does not execute steps 3-7, read source files, contact AI or operate the reviewed workflow.

## Evidence states

| State | Meaning | Must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documented mismatch | Supplied material shows a specific conflict or an overstated recorded state | Misconduct, legal liability or universal failure |
| Control concern | A mechanism may fail under identifiable conditions | That harm occurred or a person acted improperly |
| Not established | Evidence needed to decide was not supplied or could not be inspected | That the control does not exist |
| Observed in this sample | A recorded intervention works within a defined trace | That the system is safe across all conditions |

Counts describe the observation inventory, not probabilities or a composite safety rating. One card can record a proven display mismatch while explicitly leaving the actual downstream state unknown. Do not collapse those propositions into one conclusion.

## What to inspect

### Meaning and transformation

Preserve original input, transformed output, subject, negation, source span, scope and version. Separate technical parsing success from complete content acquisition. Translation fidelity, category selection and downstream action are separate checks. A second model consuming the same defective text is not automatically an independent check.

### Handoff and meaningful review

Distinguish receipt, forwarding, assignment, acknowledged responsibility, content review, response and resolution. A queue, a name or an approval click does not establish an informed decision. Ask whether the person has the evidence, skill, time, authority and practical ability to disagree. Look for a reason and an effective downstream intervention, not just a human-labelled stage.

### Training and evaluation

Separate foundation training, fine-tuning, retrieval, test data and operational feedback. Record model, prompt, rule and data versions. Historical-label agreement does not independently establish justified outcomes. Inspect selection, omitted populations, error types, uncertain labels, language/input coverage and changes over time. Keep system-generated labels out of independently verified ground truth unless independently reviewed.

### Information access and protection

Document role-specific access to originals, transformed text, exclusions, rules, model evidence and logs. Minimise personal and operational details. Distinguish a contract draft, executed terms, effective configuration, exercised inspection rights and actual retention/deletion evidence. Do not demand confidential originals where a permitted excerpt or alternative evidence can answer the question.

### Incentives and capacity

Check what staff and teams are rewarded for: speed, closures, low override rates or actual supported outcomes. Review whether a justified hold, correction, escalation or pause could disadvantage the person using it. This is a mechanism to investigate, not proof of retaliation. Include training exercises, practical workload, specialist cover and staff accounts.

### Corrections and downstream state

A correction desk is not completed remediation. Track the new version, invalidation of dependent proposals/approvals, reassessment, current authorisation, actual execution and communication. Where effects expire or can be reversed, distinguish a scheduled time, queued operation, execution and independent state read-back.

### Pause, continuity and recovery

Map the scope of a pause. New model output, queued work, active effects and later notifications may be separate. Define essential human support, affected-work reconciliation, reversals, duplicate prevention and accountable restart. Propose bounded non-production exercises, not indiscriminate production shutdowns.

### Reporting and remedy

Preserve unclassified, unclaimed, unresolved, timed-out and corrected cases. State denominators and exclusions. A report may hide a case without resolving it. Trace access to a person empowered to reconsider the outcome, including less-supported languages, disability, anonymity and concerns about challenge or retaliation.

## Domain-dependent depth

The intake begins simply; it is not an exhaustive control checklist. Relevant impact areas add questions. Financial, clinical, ESG and remedy modules are not certifications of compliance. A missing selection does not establish low impact. Privileged-access examples use common evidence/authority, privacy, continuity and remedy questions; the AI brief must still reassess the actual impact from the content.

For a real high-impact review, obtain qualified domain, operational, data-protection and legal expertise as appropriate. The responsible people, scope and evidence needed cannot be inferred solely from this example.

## Data and display

Keep stable IDs for sources, source sections, steps, transitions, selected records, claims, observations, controls and actions. Source-section references anchor exact quotations; marked spans must be contained in those quotations. Reciprocal record/observation links and every path transition are validated.

Summary shows the question and first inspection points. Flow & Cases traces selected paths and events. Controls & Access compares intended controls with observed operation. Claims retains testable accounts. Evidence holds observations, rationale and alternatives. Review Materials preserves dated source inventory; Next Checks records proposed owner, rationale and acceptance evidence.
